Welcome to S-Restaurant

S-Restaurant is a cozy, family-owned eatery located in [JAUNPUR]. We started in 2024 with a simple goal: to serve tasty food and give great service.

We cook fresh Indian dishes using local ingredients. Our food is full of flavor and made with love.

Our mission is to make every guest feel at home while enjoying delicious food.

Whether you eat here or order from home, we promise a great food experience.

This restaurant was started by Chef Shubham Kumar, who has 1+ years of cooking experience. Thanks to our customers, we’re rated 4.8 stars on Google with over 500+ happy reviews.
